Course learning outcomes

By the end of the course, you'll be able to:

CLO 1

Explain the concepts and characteristics of various computing platforms and operating environments.

CLO 2

Compare platform technologies based on organizational requirements and constraints.

CLO 3

Install and configure platform technologies for specific applications.

CLO 4

Apply platform technologies in supporting business and IT operations.

CLO 5

Evaluate platform performance and suitability for organizational needs.
